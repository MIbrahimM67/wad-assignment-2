Academics

The department offers PhD, MPhil, MS (IST), M.Sc. and BS degrees. Each degree programme has a specific objective and focus. The course of study and syllabi for each degree is updated and is inline with its objectives.

PhD

PhD is the highest degree awarded in an academic discipline. Students work closely with a faculty member, performing original research. (Learn More)

MPhil

The objective of M.Phil Computer Science programme is to train students to initiate research work. (Learn More)

MS IST

The MS programme in Information Science & Technology aims to design and develop information-related applications using IT. (Learn More)

MS DS

The MS Data Science programme equips students with the latest knowledge in data science and big data analytics. (Learn More)

BS

The BS Computer Science program provides a well-integrated balance of theoretical and practical knowledge for developing reliable software. (Learn More)
